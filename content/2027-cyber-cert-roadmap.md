# The 2027 Cyber Cert Roadmap

**Four certifications you can actually hold in your first year, in the order that works, and the honest timeline for the senior ones.**

A free guide for career changers and absolute beginners. Written by a working cybersecurity professional specializing in identity governance and access control.

Every cert fact verified 13 November 2026 against official certifying-body pages. Pricing and exam requirements change; always confirm on the certifying body's own site before you book.

---

## How to read this guide

Every page separates three different things:

- **FACT**: exam code, length, passing score, fee, experience rule, renewal requirement. Taken from the certifying body's own website, verified 13 November 2026.
- **RECOMMENDATION**: the order, the timing, when to book. That is judgement, not fact. Your situation may differ.
- **FLAGGED**: the figure is secondary-only and was not on an official page. Do not budget on it.

---

## The 30-second version

You do not need twelve certifications. You need four, in one fixed order, then a job.

The list is filtered on one question only: **can a beginner with no security job actually hold this in year one?**

| Step | Certification | Why it is here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | CompTIA Security+ (SY0-801) | No experience gate, universal recognition, the anchor for everything else |
| 2 | AWS Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02) | Cheapest exam here at 100 USD, and cloud literacy is now table stakes |
| 3 | AWS Solutions Architect Associate (SAA-C03) | Forces you to design instead of name services |
| 4 | Microsoft Identity and Access Administrator (SC-300) | Direct, no prerequisite, no SC-900 needed |
| 5 | Get a job | SOC analyst, IT support, or GRC analyst |

**Optional step zero: CompTIA Network+ (N10-009).** Only if networking is completely foreign to you. It is not in the four because it delays the anchor.

**Then, and only then, get the job.** The first-year goal is not a certification wall. It is one job: SOC analyst, IT support, or GRC analyst. Those are the roles the certifying bodies themselves name as targets, and none of them is guaranteed by any certificate. A cert qualifies you to apply. Work experience is what unlocks the senior certifications.

**Researched and kept out of 2027:** SecAI+ (cut), GRCP (cut), GRCA (cut), CGRC (cut), SC-900 (skip), PenTest+, SecurityX, AWS Security Specialty SCS-C03, Okta Administrator, SailPoint, CIDPRO, and CISSP or CISM as a certification goal. Each one has a stated reason below.

**What this guide deliberately does not do.** No job promises: a certification qualifies you to apply against the body's own stated roles, it does not hire you. No salary claims: pay depends on market, role, and location, and no badge controls that. No vendor sponsorship: every cert is judged on gate, cost, and return in year one.

## Start here: CompTIA Security+ (the anchor)

**FACT**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Certification | CompTIA Security+ |
| Current exam | SY0-801 (V8) |
| Launch | On or around 17 November 2026 |
| Previous exam | SY0-701 (launched 7 November 2023), English retires 11 June 2027 |
| Questions | Maximum 90, multiple choice and performance based |
| Duration | 90 minutes |
| Passing score | 750 on a scale of 100 to 900 |
| Formal prerequisite | None listed by CompTIA |
| Recommended experience | About two years hands on as a security administrator |
| Validity | Three years from the date you earn it |
| Renewal | CompTIA continuing education, or a qualifying higher-level cert |
| Exam fee | Not published on the certification page; secondary list price is 439 USD (FLAGGED) |

**Leads to, per CompTIA's own page:** Security Analyst, Security Engineer, Systems Administrator. The certification is DoD 8140 approved.

**What it does not do.** It does not, by itself, qualify anyone for a SOC analyst, penetration tester, or architect job. Those need the experience plus hands-on work. DoD 8140 approval is the only official job signal attached to it.

**The V8 domains, per CompTIA:**

| Domain | Weight |
|---|---|
| Security Operations | 27% |
| Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Attacks | 24% |
| Security Architecture | 19% |
| General Security Concepts | 16% |
| Security Program Management (including one objective on AI risks) | 14% |

**RECOMMENDATION: study V8 (SY0-801) from day one.** The English SY0-701 retires 11 June 2027, so anyone testing after that date has no choice. Learning the objectives once beats switching versions mid-prep. The one new thing in V8 is a single objective on the security risks associated with AI, not a whole domain.

**The version deadline, in practice.** SY0-801 and SY0-701 are the **same certification**: three-year validity, same CE renewal, no version printed on the badge employers check. There is no advantage to rushing SY0-701 before it retires, and no penalty for sitting SY0-801 after it launches. Treat V8 as the default from launch day.

**Study time to plan for:** 8 to 12 weeks at 8 to 10 hours a week if security is new to you. Faster if you already work in IT and only need the vocabulary and the exam format.

## Step 2 and Step 3: the two AWS certs

**FACT**

| Field | Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02) | Solutions Architect Associate (SAA-C03) |
|---|---|---|
| Fee | 100 USD | 150 USD |
| Format | 65 questions, 90 minutes | 65 questions, 130 minutes |
| Passing score | 700 on a scale of 100 to 1000 | 720 on a scale of 100 to 1000 |
| Validity | Three years, recertify by exam | Three years, recertify by exam |
| Prerequisite | None | None |
| Recommended experience | Up to six months of AWS exposure, explicitly not required | One or more years designing solutions on AWS |

AWS states that all AWS Certifications may be earned without completing specific prerequisites. There is no annual fee.

**What each one buys you.** Cloud Practitioner is foundational vocabulary only and does not qualify for a cloud or security job alone. Solutions Architect Associate is about 30 percent security content: it is an architecture cert, not a security cert, and that is the point. It is the exam that makes you design instead of name services.

**RECOMMENDATION: do both, in that order.** CLF-C02 is the cheapest exam on this roadmap and the fastest to pass. SAA-C03 costs more and takes longer, but it changes what you can be trusted to do. Studying SAA-C03 first is possible and usually slower, because it assumes the vocabulary CLF-C02 gives you.

**Study load, at 8 to 10 hours a week:** CLF-C02, 4 to 6 weeks. SAA-C03, 8 to 12 weeks, the heaviest of the four. If you have never opened the AWS console, spend one hands-on week before you start reading.

### The 50 percent voucher: what is verified, and what is not

**FACT, verified on an official AWS page.** A 50 percent discount voucher exists. The AWS Benefits page says you get one to apply toward recertification or any other exam. Benefits appear in your AWS Certification Account, within five business days of passing.

**FLAGGED, not verified on any official page.** That each pass earns a new voucher. That the voucher lasts one year. The single-use terms, and that expiry cannot be extended. Third-party guides assert all three as fact without an official citation, and two careful reviews of the AWS pages say the pages do not state them.

**RECOMMENDATION: budget the list price.** The certain cost is **550 USD** for CLF-C02, SAA-C03, and SCS-C03 together. The maximum saving is **225 USD** if the voucher chain works exactly as the unverified guides describe. Budget 550 USD. Treat the 225 USD as a maybe, never as a guarantee. Plan as if the voucher exists, is single-use, and shows an expiry in your own account.

**Do the math on year one.** CLF-C02 plus SAA-C03 is 250 USD at list. That is the whole cloud spend for 2027. AWS Security Specialty is a 2028 or later purchase, after you are working in a cloud.

## Step 4: Microsoft SC-300, and why SC-900 is skipped

**FACT**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Certification | Microsoft Certified: Identity and Access Administrator Associate |
| Exam | SC-300 |
| Fee | 165 USD, varies by country and region |
| Passing score | 700 on a scale of 1000 |
| Prerequisite | None. SC-900 is not required |
| Audience familiarity | Azure, Microsoft 365 workloads, Active Directory Domain Services, PowerShell, Kusto Query Language |
| Validity | One year, renewed by a free online assessment, no exam retake |

**RECOMMENDATION: take SC-300 directly after Security+.** Microsoft lists no prerequisite for SC-300, and none for SC-900 either, so nothing is unlocked by taking SC-900 first. After Security+, SC-900 is largely duplicative: broad security, compliance, and identity concepts that Security+ has just tested harder. It is useful only as a cheap Microsoft-specific vocabulary check.

**SC-900, for the record.** No prerequisite, around 45 minutes, passing score 700, 99 USD and no expiry per the Microsoft price table as reported by secondary sources (FLAGGED: the official SC-900 page was not opened live in this research).

## The identity certs: one hard chain, two gates

### Okta Certified Professional, then Okta Certified Administrator

**FACT.** Okta Professional has no prerequisite. Fee is 250 USD for a first attempt and 100 USD for a retake. The exam is 15 discrete-option multiple-choice questions plus 4 performance use cases over 150 minutes. Recommended experience is one year in a technical security or identity role plus three months hands-on Okta. Validity is two years, or three years if you also hold Technical Architect.

**FACT: the one hard chain.** Okta Professional is a **prerequisite** for Okta Certified Administrator. Okta's own prep guide states that an active Professional certification is required, and it must be active at registration. Administrator is 250 USD, valid two years. Recommended experience for Administrator is about two years in role and one year with Okta (FLAGGED, secondary only).

**RECOMMENDATION: only if your target employers name Okta.** Both Okta exams are hands-on in a live Okta org. Without about three months of Okta access through a job, lab, or tenant, Professional is not a realistic first cert. Take SC-300 instead unless the platform is named in the job posts you are actually applying to.

### SailPoint: no prerequisite, but employer gated

**FACT.** SailPoint's official Policy Handbook lists the prerequisite as None for both the Identity Security Administrator and IdentityIQ Engineer certifications, with a two-year validity. Both recommend six to twelve months of hands-on product time. Fees are not in the handbook at all (FLAGGED).

**RECOMMENDATION: later, employer gated.** Exams are purchasable only inside Identity University, which is gated, and preparation needs a product tenant. A beginner with no job cannot meaningfully prepare. Revisit once you are inside an IAM team.

### IDPro CIDPRO: holdable, poor return

**FACT.** No prerequisite and no experience requirement stated on the official FAQ. Fee is 750 USD. About 150 questions, 3 hours, online proctored. No validity period and no continuing education stated at this time.

**RECOMMENDATION: later.** Poor value in 2027 against SC-300 at 165 USD or Okta Professional at 250 USD. It is a purchase for someone already in an identity program role, not a first credential.

**If you can only pick one identity cert, pick SC-300.** It is the most common IAM requirement in job posts, the cheapest of the four, and it has no gate.

## Researched, deferred: not wrong, just early

Every cert below is real, and every one has no experience gate to hold. They are off the 2027 list because the recommended experience describes work you do not have yet, so the return in year one is poor.

| Certification | Fee (USD) | Recommended experience | Status | Why it waits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CompTIA CySA+ (CS0-004, launched 23 June 2026) | 439 secondary (FLAGGED) | About four years as a SOC or vulnerability analyst | Later | Builds on Security+, not a repeat: security operations 34%, vulnerability management 26%, incident response 24%, reporting 16%. Do it as your first cert inside a SOC job |
| CompTIA PenTest+ (PT0-003) | 439 secondary (FLAGGED) | Three to four years as a penetration tester, with Network+ and Security+ knowledge | Later | Offensive work is a second job, not a first one. Do not take CySA+ and PenTest+ back to back with no job |
| CompTIA SecurityX (CAS-005, formerly CASP+) | Not found (FLAGGED) | Ten years of IT, including five in security | Later | Senior capstone, pass or fail with no scaled score. A five-year horizon item |
| AWS Security Specialty (SCS-C03) | 300 | Disputed: three to five years securing cloud, or five years IT security plus two on AWS | Later | Not straight after CLF-C02 with no security or AWS work. Do SAA-C03 first |
| Okta Certified Administrator (OKADM1) | 250 | About two years in role and one year with Okta (FLAGGED) | Chained | Requires an active Okta Professional first |
| SailPoint Administrator / Engineer | Not in handbook (FLAGGED) | Six to twelve months hands on the product | Gated | Exams only purchasable inside Identity University, and prep needs a tenant |
| IDPro CIDPRO | 750 | None stated on the official FAQ | Later | No job gate and no continuing education, at three times the price of SC-300 |

**The rule behind this list.** Pass equals certified is not the same as ready. None of these has an experience gate, so technically you can sit any of them next month. The recommended experience is the body telling you what the exam assumes, and paying 300 to 750 USD to sit an exam whose vocabulary you have never used at work is the most common way beginners waste a year.

## Researched and cut: five that are off this roadmap

These were in earlier drafts. Each one was checked against its certifying body and rejected for a stated reason, so the cut is on the record.

### CompTIA SecAI+ (CY0-001): cut

**Not a duplicate, but premature.** 40 percent of the exam is securing AI systems: prompt injection, jailbreaks, model extraction, training data poisoning, adversarial ML. None of that is in Security+. The other 60 percent re-applies known Security+ topics through an AI lens. CompTIA calls it an expansion certification and its FAQ says it is not for individuals entering cybersecurity for the first time. It assumes Security+ or CySA+ level knowledge and two or more years of work. Exam facts: launched 17 February 2026, maximum 60 questions, 60 minutes, passing score 600, 15 CEUs to renew, fee 298 to 359 USD secondary (FLAGGED).

**Revisit after two years of cybersecurity work, or in an AI-facing role.** Employers will not credit an AI specialism without a security foundation and a job. The 2027 priority is CySA+ inside a SOC, or getting the job first.

### OCEG GRCP and GRCA: cut

**Largely redundant for the goal that matters.** They teach OCEG's GRC Capability Model, which is not ISACA's or ISC2's body of knowledge, and they earn no waiver or experience credit toward CISA, CISM, or CISSP. No experience gate, open book, 100 questions, 120 minutes, 70 percent to pass, bundled into the 499 USD a year All Access Pass (399 renewal). GRCA lists GRCP as strongly recommended, not required, per the certification page; an OCEG article says otherwise (FLAGGED, contradiction stands).

**Verdict:** useful study material for GRC vocabulary, weak as a credential. The vocabulary can come from the labs and the work itself. Do not present either as progress toward CISA, CISM, or CISSP.

### ISC2 CGRC: cut

**Not redundant in content, but narrow and gated.** 599 USD exam plus 135 USD a year maintenance, 125 questions, 3 hours, passing score 700 of 1000. Holding it needs two years of cumulative full-time work in one of the seven domains, with no degree waiver published. Its core is system authorization and RMF, valuable mainly in federal, defense, or RMF roles.

**The CISSP waiver.** CGRC is reported to waive one year of CISSP, from the ISC2 approved credential list revised 1 April 2026. That list was not opened live in this research, so treat the waiver as reported, not verified. Not a reason to buy it.

### Microsoft SC-900: skip

**Duplicative after Security+.** Microsoft lists no prerequisite for SC-900 or SC-300, so nothing is unlocked by taking SC-900 first. It covers broad security, compliance, and identity concepts that Security+ has just tested harder. If you already hold Security+, put the money toward SC-300.

**What replaced all five: nothing.** That is the point. The route to a well-paid GRC or audit role does not run through a pile of GRC certificates. It runs through work, then CISA, CISM, or CISSP. None of the five above shortens that, and none waives ISACA or ISC2 experience.

## CISA, CISM, CISSP: years, not months

All three exams can be sat early. None can be **held** without real work experience, and none is a 2027 goal for someone starting from zero. The clock below is work years, not study years, and it assumes a relevant job starts in 2027.

| Goal | Fee (USD) | Work required to hold it | Earliest certification |
|---|---|---|---|
| ISACA CISA | 575 member / 760 non-member, plus 50 application, plus 45 or 85 a year | 5 years of IS audit, control, assurance, or security work in the last 10 years, minimum 2 of them direct in a CISA domain. Waivers capped at 3 years. A bachelor's in any field counts 2 years; a master's in Information Systems counts 3 | About 2030, at 3 years of work. About 2029 with a master's in IS. With no waiver at all: 2032 |
| ISC2 CISSP | 749 in the Americas and APAC, plus 135 a year AMF | 5 years cumulative paid work in at least 2 of the 8 domains. Only 1 year can be waived, by a degree in computer science or IT on the official page, or by an approved credential such as Security+. Not both | About 2031, at 4 years of work with the one-year waiver. About 2032 at 5 years if no waiver applies |
| ISACA CISM | 575 member / 760 non-member, plus 50 application, plus 45 or 85 a year | 5 years of information security management experience, with at least 3 years in 3 of the 4 domains, and that part cannot be waived. Waivers capped at 2 years. A generic bachelor's waives 0 years for CISM; Security+ waives 1 | Realistically 2032: 5 years, or 4 with Security+. Only if you are managing people or a program from about 2027 |

**CISM is not a three-years-from-zero cert.** Management experience is the gate, not just years served. Do CISA first, then CISM only after you manage people or a security program. Do not schedule CISM before that, and do not take CISA and CISM as a pair in the same year.

**Sitting early, and the expiry trap.** You may sit any of the three before you meet the experience requirement, but a pass banked in 2027 has to become a certification application within five years (ISACA), or become full CISSP within six years as an Associate of ISC2. With no job lined up, the pass can lapse before the experience is earned.

**The CISA waivers, in official wording.** Five years of IS audit, control, assurance, or security work experience, with a minimum of two years from one of the five CISA job practice domains. One year of IS experience or financial audit work counts one year. Education waivers, only one may be applied: an associate degree, the IT Audit Fundamentals cert, or CCAK counts one year; a bachelor's, master's, or doctorate in any field counts two years; a master's in Information Systems or a related field counts three years. Total waivers are capped at three years, and the two years of direct experience cannot be waived.

**The CISM waivers, in official wording.** Five years of experience in the information security management field, with experience in 3 of the 4 domain areas, and a minimum of 3 years in 3 of the 4 job practice areas. Substitutions, only one may be applied: a current CISA or CISSP counts two years; an MBA or master's in Information Security counts two years; a bachelor's in Information Security or a skills-based cert such as Security+ counts one year. A generic four-year degree is not on the CISM waiver list.

**The CISSP waiver and the Associate route.** Five years cumulative full-time experience in two or more of the eight domains. A post-secondary degree in computer science, IT, or related fields may satisfy up to one year, or an approved ISC2 credential may satisfy up to one year, and only one year can be waived. Pass the exam without the experience and you become an Associate of ISC2 with six years to earn the five years; Associate AMF is 50 USD a year against 135 USD for a full CISSP. For a non-IT degree the waiver is not verified (FLAGGED).

### Sequencing that follows from the evidence

This is sequencing, not a promise of employment.

- **2027.** Security+ SY0-801, then CLF-C02, then SAA-C03, then SC-300. Then get any SOC, IT support, or GRC analyst job. No GRCP: the GRC vocabulary comes from the labs and the work itself.
- **2027 to 2029.** Work in audit, GRC, or RMF as it is available. Sit the CISA exam when ready, because a pass is banked for five years. No CGRC on the way: go straight for CISA.
- **About 2030.** CISA, at three years of work with the bachelor's waiver.
- **2030 to 2031.** CISSP exam, then Associate of ISC2, then full CISSP at four years of work.
- **2032 onward.** CISM, once you hold three years of management experience.

## The 2027 calendar

**This is a recommendation, not a rule.** It assumes roughly 10 hours a week. Move the dates; keep the order.

**Q1 2027: Security+ (SY0-801).** Sit it by the end of March. Study the V8 objectives from day one. Do not start studying for a second cert before this one is passed.

**Q2 2027: AWS Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02).** 100 USD, the shortest exam on the list. Book it before you feel ready. Pass mark 700 of 1000.

**Q3 2027: AWS Solutions Architect Associate (SAA-C03).** The hard one. 130 minutes and real design questions. This is the cert that changes what you can be trusted to do.

**Q4 2027: Microsoft SC-300.** Direct, no SC-900. Then job applications in earnest: SOC Tier 1, IT support, GRC analyst.

**All year: two lab projects, documented.** One detection or incident writeup. One cloud, identity, or control-mapping build. Certifications get you the interview; the writeups get you the offer. If you cannot explain what you built and why, it does not count.

### If you have zero IT experience

**RECOMMENDATION.** Get any technical job first, including help desk, desktop support, or a NOC role, and take it while you study. The certification will not compensate for having never worked a ticket, and the ticket job is what gives you the stories you will tell in interviews.

### If you already work in IT

**RECOMMENDATION.** Skip the foundational certs and go straight at Security+. Then pick cloud or identity based on the team you want to transfer into. Internal moves are the least competitive route into security, and your current manager is the cheapest sponsor you will ever find.

### Five rules that decide whether this works

1. One certification at a time. Starting three is how people finish none.
2. Book the exam date before you feel ready. A date creates urgency that a study plan cannot.
3. Lab everything you read. If you cannot show it, you do not know it.
4. Read the official exam objectives, not just a course. The objectives are the exam.
5. Budget for renewal. Every CompTIA cert here expires in three years, and SC-300 renews every 12 months.

### What not to do in 2027

SecAI+, GRCP, GRCA, CGRC, SC-900, PenTest+, SecurityX, AWS Security Specialty SCS-C03, Okta Administrator, SailPoint Administrator or Engineer, CIDPRO at 750 USD, and CISSP or CISM as a certification goal.

CompTIA does not publish a single list price on its certification pages, and voucher prices vary with bundles, retakes, and region. Budget a few hundred US dollars per attempt and confirm the number at checkout on comptia.org. Academic pricing, exam bundles, and employer reimbursement are the three legitimate ways to reduce it.

## Open questions: what could not be verified

A figure that is not on an official page stays flagged here rather than being promoted into the roadmap as fact. Nothing above depends on an item below.

1. **AWS 50% voucher duration and per-pass renewal.** Not on the official Benefits page opened live. Third-party guides assert one year and a new voucher per pass; two careful reviews say the AWS pages do not state it. Unverified.
2. **AWS SCS-C03 recommended experience.** Three to five years securing cloud, against five years of IT security plus two on AWS, from sources that both cite AWS. Unresolved.
3. **CompTIA exam prices.** 439 and 298 were not verified live on the CompTIA store. SecAI+ appeared at 359 in some 2026 sources and 298 in October 2026. No SecurityX price was found at all.
4. **SailPoint exam fees.** Not in the official Policy Handbook, because Identity University is gated. Secondary figures conflict.
5. **CISSP degree waiver for a generic, non-IT degree.** The ISC2 page says computer science, IT, or related fields; secondary guides say any field. Do not assume the waiver.
6. **CISA bachelor's waiver field.** The ISACA Support page says any field, worth two years. Some secondary sources say IT or accounting only. Follow the Support page, and re-check the live CISA application before counting on it.
7. **Okta Administrator recommended experience**, about two years in role and one year with Okta. Secondary only.
8. **SC-900 fee and no-expiry status.** Verified through the Microsoft price table as reported by secondary sources, not by a live open of the SC-900 page.
9. **CIDPRO experience and validity.** The official FAQ states neither. The claims of two or more years of experience, and of no expiry, are secondary only.
10. **GRCA prerequisite.** The OCEG certification page says recommended, not required. An OCEG Top 10 article says GRCP in good standing is required. The contradiction stands.
11. **CGRC waiving one year of CISSP.** Reported from the ISC2 approved credential list revised 1 April 2026, which was not opened live. Reported, not verified.
12. **What a cert qualifies a beginner for.** No certifying body guarantees a job or a role. The role mappings here are the bodies' own stated target roles, not employment promises.
